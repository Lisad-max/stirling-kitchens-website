A kitchen built around your brief, not a template

A kitchen that works well starts with listening properly. Craig will sit down with you, understand how the space is actually used — where people gather, how many people cook at once, what drives you mad about your current layout — and design something around your reality.

Everything is then manufactured in our East Tāmaki workshop. We don't source cabinets from a third party and clip them together on-site. Wayne and the team cut and build every component in-house, which means we can accommodate unusual dimensions, difficult corners, and non-standard ceiling heights that off-the-shelf products can't handle.

The result is a kitchen that fits your home properly — from the first measurement to the day Stacy and the team complete the installation.

How it works

From conversation to completed kitchen

Three clear stages, with the same team involved from start to finish.

Design consultation

Craig meets with you in your home to understand the space, your brief, and how you actually use the kitchen. You'll get a design that solves the practical problems most people don't think to ask about until it's too late to fix them.

Workshop manufacturing

Every component is cut and built by Wayne and the team in our East Tāmaki workshop. In-house manufacturing means we control the quality at every step and can accommodate dimensions that standard products can't.

Professional installation

Stacy and the installation team fit the kitchen properly — level, plumb, and finished to a standard that holds up. No shortcuts, no rushing. The kind of job you notice in the small things years later.
