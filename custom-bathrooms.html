A bathroom that's been thought through properly

A well-designed bathroom vanity does more than hold the sink. It determines how much storage you have, how well the space flows, and whether the finish holds up through years of steam and daily use.

We build custom bathroom vanities and cabinetry to fit the actual dimensions of your bathroom — not the standard sizes that force a compromise on space or style. Whether it's a single vanity in a compact ensuite, a double-sink vanity in a main bathroom, or built-in storage around a bathtub, we can size and finish it exactly as required.

Material choices, drawer configurations, and surface finishes are chosen to suit how the bathroom is used — and how long you plan to live with it. Everything is manufactured in our East Tāmaki workshop, the same way we build our kitchens.

What we manufacture

Custom to your bathroom, not a catalogue

Every piece is designed and built for your specific space. A few of the things people commonly ask us to build:

🚿

Vanity units

Single and double-sink vanities in any width, depth and height — built to fit the exact dimensions of your bathroom, including awkward alcoves and non-standard walls.

🪞

Storage and shelving

Built-in bathroom storage, medicine cabinets, linen cupboards and towel nooks — sized and placed to make the space work, not just look good in a photo.

🪵

Timber and veneer finishes

Warm timber tones — oak, walnut, and others — as well as painted finishes and stone benchtops. All selected to hold up in a bathroom environment.

📐

Non-standard sizes

If your bathroom doesn't fit standard product dimensions, we can build it to fit. That's the practical reason to go custom rather than catalogue.
