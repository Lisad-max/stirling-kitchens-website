The business

Built from the workshop up

Stirling Kitchens has been designing and manufacturing custom kitchens for Auckland homes for over 25 years. The business is built around a simple idea: if you design something properly, manufacture it in-house, and install it with care, you don't need to oversell it. The work speaks for itself.

Everything we make comes out of our workshop in East Tāmaki. That means we control the quality at every stage — there's no component sourced from a supplier we've never met, and no handoff to a contractor we can't vouch for. The team that designs your kitchen is the same team that builds it and installs it.

We work with homeowners renovating or building, and with builders and developers who need a manufacturer they can rely on to deliver on time and to spec. Either way, the process starts the same way: a conversation with Craig about what you're trying to achieve.

The team

The people behind the work

A small team with a lot of experience between them. Everyone knows what they're doing and why it matters.

Craig

Designer

Twenty-five years in the industry has given Craig an eye for kitchen design that can't be taught in a classroom. He's sat across the table from hundreds of families over the decades, listening to how they actually live — where the school bags land, who does the cooking, what drives everyone mad about their old layout — and turned it into a design that works from the very first morning. That depth of knowledge means Craig isn't just drawing a pretty kitchen; he's solving the practical problems most people don't think to ask about until it's too late to fix them.

Stacy

Kitchen Installer

With 18 years spent fitting kitchens into real homes — not showrooms — Stacy has the patience and precision that only comes from doing the job properly, thousands of times over. Every cut, every fixing, every levelled cabinet is done the way she'd want it done in her own home. Customers notice it in the small things: the drawers that glide shut without a nudge, the join lines you have to look hard to find.

How we work

What we think matters

👂

Start with listening

A good kitchen design comes from understanding how people actually use the space — not from showing clients a catalogue and asking them to pick. The consultation is where the real work starts.

🔧

Made in-house

We don't source cabinets from an overseas supplier and clip them together. Every component is manufactured by Wayne in our workshop — which means we control the quality and can accommodate what standard products can't.

🤝

Same team, start to finish

The person who designs your kitchen is involved in the project through to installation. There's no handoff to a subcontractor who doesn't know the brief.

✅

Honest about what's involved

We'll tell you upfront what a project will take, what it will cost, and what's realistic for your timeline. No surprises partway through.

Come and talk to the team

The first step is a conversation. Call Craig and tell him what you're thinking — he'll give you a straight answer about what's possible.
