## 5. Featured page — Custom Furniture, leaning into Booth & Banquette Seating

This replaces a plain "Custom Furniture" page. It keeps the Custom Furniture name/nav label but the content, imagery, and hero focus lead with booth/banquette seating as the specialty, since that's the higher-intent trend to capture. General custom furniture work can still get a shorter mention further down the page.

- **Why this page leads with booth seating:** Banquette/booth seating is one of the most talked-about kitchen trends going into 2026 — design press (Homes & Gardens and others) is calling it one of the most coveted kitchen features of the year, prized for turning the kitchen into a proper gathering spot rather than just a workspace. Stirling Kitchens specialises in building it, so leading with it on the Custom Furniture page positions the business ahead of the trend and captures the search intent directly.
- **Primary message:** Booth/banquette seating is where conversation happens — a built-in, custom-made seating nook that makes the kitchen the room everyone gathers in.
- **Content outline:**
  1. H1: "Custom Furniture" with a sub-headline leading on the trend (e.g. "Including our specialty — custom booth & banquette seating, the built-in nook where conversation happens")
  2. Brief explanation of what banquette/booth seating is and why it's trending — cosy, space-saving, sociable, built-in storage underneath
  3. Primary call to action: **Contact Us** (matches the primary site CTA — call or enquire)
  4. Why choose Stirling Kitchens for it — custom-built to fit awkward corners, bay windows, kitchen islands; made in-house, not off-the-shelf
  5. Layout options — L-shaped, U-shaped, curved, island-integrated (per current design trend variations)
  6. Gallery/photo section using the three reference images in `Booth Seating Images/` (clearly styled as inspiration, not yet Stirling Kitchens' own completed projects — see Section 3)
  7. Shorter section: other custom furniture the business builds beyond booth seating (general description, expand later with real project photos)
  8. Final call to action: **Contact Us** to design a custom booth seating nook or furniture piece
- **Primary CTA:** Contact Us (call or enquiry form) — consistent with the site's main conversion action
- **Internal links:** Link to/from Custom Kitchens page and Our Work/Gallery

## 7. Website objectives

- **Primary business objective:** Generate phone enquiries from homeowners and trade clients for custom kitchens (and secondarily bathrooms and custom furniture/booth seating)
- **Most important visitor action:** Call the business
- **Secondary action:** Send an enquiry/contact form for visitors not ready to call
- **Target audience:** Homeowners renovating/building; builders and developers
- **Audience questions/concerns likely to address:** What does Stirling Kitchens make, is it fully custom, what's the process, how experienced is the team, what areas do they serve, how do I get a quote
- **Reasons to choose this business:** 30 years combined experience, custom manufacturing (not just supply), a specialty in the booth/banquette seating trend
