About us

Built in East Tāmaki, delivered to Auckland homes

Stirling Kitchens has been building custom kitchens for Auckland homes for over 25 years. We manufacture everything ourselves from our workshop in East Tāmaki — which means every kitchen, bathroom vanity or piece of furniture that leaves our doors was made to order, not sourced from a catalogue.

There are no sub-contractors or middlemen. The same team that designs your kitchen builds it. When Craig has sat down with you and designed something around how your family actually lives, it's Wayne and the team in the workshop who then turn that design into the real thing — exactly as drawn.
